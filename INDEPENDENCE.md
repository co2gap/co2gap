# Independence, right of reply, and paid work

This project names airports, routes and air navigation service providers. Some
of them are, in principle, the same organisations that might one day pay for a
detailed analysis of their own traffic. That is a real conflict of interest, and
the only useful response is a rule written down **before** any money exists,
narrow enough to be checked.

## Current status

**As of 1 September 2026, no organisation named on this site has any commercial
relationship with this project, and no work has been paid for by anyone.** The
project has no revenue, no funding and no sponsor. This paragraph is part of
each release and is updated, not removed, if that ever changes.

## The five rules

**1. The public figure is never a deliverable.**
What could be sold is analysis *of* a published figure — the breakdown behind
it, by hour, origin, aircraft type and month. Never a change *to* it. No
customer can commission a recalculation whose result is a different public
number.

**2. No advance access, embargo or preview for anyone, customers included.**
Where organisations are notified before publication, *every* named organisation
is notified, on the same terms and with the same lead time, and notification
confers no ability to alter what is published. Advance notice is a matter of
fairness, never a commercial courtesy.

**3. Right of reply is free, unconditional and published in full.**
Anyone named here may reply. The reply is published next to the figure it
concerns, unedited, whether or not the sender is a customer, and whether or not
they have paid for anything. If a reply demonstrates an error, the figure is
corrected and the correction is dated.

**4. A commercial relationship with a named organisation is disclosed next to
that organisation's figure.**
If an airport, airline or ANSP named on this site becomes a customer, its entry
says so, permanently and in the same typeface as the number. This is cheap to
promise while there are no customers and impossible to introduce later without
it reading as a confession — which is exactly why it is written now.

**5. What is published is measured quantities, never a grade or a score.**
No letter grades, no tiers, no composite index, no league table. The site
publishes physical quantities — kilograms of CO₂, percentage distance from a
stated optimum — with their uncertainty and with an explicit statement of where
the ordering stops being meaningful. Anything that compresses an organisation
into a single mark is outside what this method can support, and is not offered
at any price.

## Which structure this is

There are two ways to run something that measures organisations:

- **A third party pays.** The rated organisation is never the customer; revenue
  comes from someone else. This is how NewsGuard operates: it does not charge
  publishers to be rated, and sells to platforms and advertisers instead. The
  conflict is structurally absent, and the willingness to pay is lower.
- **The measured organisation pays.** This is how airline emissions rankings and
  ESG ratings work. Revenue is higher and the conflict is real — real enough
  that it is the reason those sectors ended up regulated. It survives scrutiny
  only through explicit conflict rules and, at scale, third-party assurance.

This project, if it ever earns anything from the organisations it measures, is
in the second category. The rules above exist because of that, not in spite of
it. A broad claim of impartiality is worth nothing: *Consumer Reports* buys
every product at retail and refuses advertising, and is still criticised over
referral fees. Only narrow, checkable commitments survive.

## What would have to change before charging anyone

- A legal entity capable of invoicing, which does not exist today.
- A written statement, published here, of who pays for what.
- Independent review of the methodology by someone qualified to judge it — the
  substitute, at this scale, for the formal assurance that larger providers buy.

Until all three exist, the answer to any commercial enquiry is that the work is
not for sale yet.

## Reporting a problem

`hello@co2gap.org` — corrections, replies and objections to any figure. If you
believe this policy has been broken, that address is also the right one, and the
complaint will be published alongside the response to it.
